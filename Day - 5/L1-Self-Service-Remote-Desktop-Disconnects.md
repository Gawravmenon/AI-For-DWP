# Work Screen Keeps Disconnecting - Quick Help Guide

Version header: v 1.0 | 07/08/2026 | Status: Draft

If your work screen keeps dropping and coming back, follow these steps. You can do all of them safely by yourself.

Your files are safe. This problem affects your connection only. It does not remove your documents.

## What you can do now

1. Save your work if you can.
If the screen still responds, save any open files.

2. Sign out of the work screen.
Use your picture at the top right, or use the Start menu sign-out option.

3. Wait for 2 minutes.
This gives IT time to place your next sign-in on a working computer.

4. Sign in again.
Open your usual work screen app and connect as normal.

5. If it drops again, sign out and sign in one more time.
Sign out, wait 2 minutes, then sign in again.

6. If needed, restart your own laptop or desktop.
After restart, open the work screen app and sign in again.

## When to contact the Service Desk

Contact the Service Desk if the problem still happens after two sign-ins and one restart of your own computer.

## Details to include in your help request

Please share these details in your help request or chat:
- Your name and work username
- Best phone number to reach you
- Time the problem started (include your local time)
- How many times the screen dropped
- Exact message shown on screen (if any)
- Your computer name (if you know it)
- Your location (office or home) and internet type (office Wi-Fi, home Wi-Fi, or phone hotspot)
- Whether coworkers have the same problem
- Last time it worked normally

## Reassurance

Your documents and settings are not lost. The support team can restore stable access quickly once they have the details above.
