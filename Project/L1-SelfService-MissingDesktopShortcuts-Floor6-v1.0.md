If desktop shortcuts disappear, first sign out and sign back in once. If shortcuts are still missing, do not recreate many shortcuts yet, because we need to confirm whether files were moved or removed.

Report to the Service Desk with: "Floor 6 missing shortcuts", your device name, and examples of missing apps. This helps us compare affected and unaffected devices quickly.

What to expect: we will check whether shortcuts were relocated by profile sync or changed by recent package or policy updates. We will provide updates after each validation checkpoint rather than promise a fixed time.
