If your Floor 6 laptop takes a very long time to sign in, or sign-in appears stuck, do one restart only and keep the device connected to power and network. Please avoid repeated restart loops, because that can extend background processing.

If you are still blocked after one restart, contact the Service Desk and include: "Floor 6 login priority", your device name, and your callback number. We are currently isolating affected devices from a recent app rollout while we validate the fix path.

What to expect: you may see gradual improvement as device assignments refresh. We will post updates as checkpoints are confirmed; we are not publishing a fixed resolution time until validation is complete.
