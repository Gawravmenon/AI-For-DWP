If you cannot sign in, first restart your laptop and try again once connected to a stable internet connection. If it still fails, make sure Caps Lock is off and re-enter your password slowly.

Contact the Service Desk if you still cannot sign in after these steps or if your account shows locked. Share: your username, exact time of the last failed attempt, device name, and a screenshot/photo of the message.

If OneDrive files look missing, open OneDrive from the taskbar and confirm you are signed in with your work account. Then click Sync and wait 2-3 minutes, and check OneDrive on the web to see if files are there.

Contact the Service Desk if files are still missing after this check. Share: file or folder names, last time you saw them, whether they appear on web but not laptop, and a screenshot of any warning.

If the S: drive will not open, disconnect and reconnect your VPN, then sign out of Windows and sign back in once. After login, try opening S: again from File Explorer.

Contact the Service Desk if S: is still unavailable. Share: your username, exact error text, time of last attempt, whether teammates can access it, and the folder path you were trying to open.
