# Leadership Executive Summary
Date: 2026-08-12

Overall, the Windows 11 migration has gone well for most end users. The early issues that affected day-to-day work were identified quickly, and several of the biggest problems were already improving by the next round of feedback. In plain terms, the main experience is moving in the right direction: users are reporting smoother sign-in, better reliability, and fewer complaints about missing files or connection problems. There are still some adjustment points, but the general picture is that the rollout is settling down and most users are able to work normally.

What remains open is focused and clear. The main outstanding issue is a small number of specific work blockers, especially around printing on floor 3 and a newer issue affecting large spreadsheet files. Those are being treated as priority items, with targeted follow-up rather than a broad reset. We are also keeping a close watch on any return of sign-in or file access problems so they can be handled early. The next step is continued monitoring, direct user updates where needed, and fast service desk action on any cases that stop people getting work done.
