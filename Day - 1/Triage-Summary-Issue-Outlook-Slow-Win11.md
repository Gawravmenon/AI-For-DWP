## Impact (who/how many/ business urgency)
- Who: Single end user (to confirm)
- How many: One reported device/user (to confirm)
- Business urgency: User cannot access Outlook/email function; urgency level to confirm

- Exact time issue began and whether issue is constant or intermittent.

- Any error message/code/screenshots from Outlook or Windows.

- Whether issue affects Outlook Web App as a workaround.
- Available disk space, CPU/RAM usage at time of slowness, and background update activity.
- Whether other users with similar new Windows 11 builds are affected.

## likely catagory
- Likely category: Endpoint performance and application issue (Outlook on Windows 11)
- To confirm: Service request vs incident classification under local process

## Suggest first diagnostic step
